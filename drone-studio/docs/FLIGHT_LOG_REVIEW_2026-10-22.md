# First wobbly Stabilize flight — onboard log review

The operator confirmed that onboard log **10** is the flight in which the 3.5-inch quad made a fast shiver/buzz that worsened as throttle rose. The operator also reported that motors and electronics felt similarly cool afterward. Lucidity downloaded the original 409,746-byte ArduPilot DataFlash `.BIN` over direct flight-controller USB while the aircraft was disarmed, battery unplugged, and all propellers removed. A local copy is `artifacts/flight-logs/2026-10-22-wobbly-flight-log10.BIN` (SHA-256 `8e45e26aacb0979960f3d0ec3698b28674f626ce8ebccea0fb7ac0f8e856029c`). `artifacts/` is ignored by Git; this is local flight data and should not be published without review. No aircraft command or parameter write was made during this analysis.

The recorded roll/pitch rate P/I/D parameters were `0.135/0.135/0.0036`, with 20 Hz gyro/rate filters and both harmonic notches disabled. Those are observations, not a tuning prescription. There are no ESC RPM or high-rate raw gyro samples in this file. The Lucidity parser flagged an incomplete trailing record, so interpret the logged summaries as evidence from the parsed span, not as an exhaustive integrity claim.

**Next action:** keep the aircraft grounded and battery disconnected. Recheck each prop's orientation and retention on its smooth shaft, motor/arm screws, motor bells, FC stack mounting, and whether any wire or antenna touches the FC or prop disk. Do not use the automated storyboard to test through the shiver. After the physical inspection, use ArduPilot's supervised initial-tuning process with the experienced pilot; choose any parameter changes deliberately and verify them one at a time. A stable pilot-controlled Stabilize hover must precede AltHold, Loiter, and a Lucidity-launched mission.
