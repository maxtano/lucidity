# One-drone mission launch gate

**Status (October 21, 2026): guarded physical launch, monitor, LAND and props-off recovery are implemented in the local Lucidity service and Flight readiness UI; the actual quad has not flown a Lucidity mission.** The fixed command transaction passed fresh-wipe Copter 4.7.1 SITL normal, explicit LAND, missing START ACK, link-loss/unknown and two-flight repeat scenarios through an injected loopback adapter. Middleware, UI and service checks with injected fixtures exercise product paths but are not a flight test of the quad, DroneBridge or Zorro. This is the gate for the 2 m **First live hover** candidate, not field clearance.

## What SITL actually proved

In isolated ArduCopter 4.7.1 SITL, Lucidity's service uploaded and read back the exact saved template, `HOME → TAKEOFF (2 m) → LOITER_TIME (5 s) → LAND`, from both empty and home-only starting missions. A separate SITL flight armed in Stabilize, then sent `MAV_CMD_MISSION_START`; Copter entered AUTO, reported `MISSION_CURRENT` 0→1→2→3, peaked at 2.122 m relative to home, held near 1.99 m, landed, and reported disarmed after about 18.6 s. Another run switched AUTO→LOITER at about 1.215 m, then LAND and disarmed. A disarmed mission-current reset permitted a repeat run; a separate eight-second telemetry disconnect showed the mission continuing in SITL with GCS failsafe disabled. Merely switching to AUTO after arming did **not** start takeoff under default `AUTO_OPTIONS=0`; the vehicle auto-disarmed after about 10 s. These are simulator observations, not real-aircraft evidence. The [evaluation record](SITL_MISSION_EVALUATION_2026-10-21.md) has the exact cases and limits.

ArduCopter 4.7.1's `MAV_CMD_MISSION_START` handler rejects nonzero first/last-item arguments, switches to AUTO, sets `auto_armed`, and starts or resumes the mission. Lucidity uses `COMMAND_LONG` for `MAV_CMD_COMPONENT_ARM_DISARM` (`400`, arm=1, force=0) **while still in Stabilize, AltHold or Loiter**, then `MAV_CMD_MISSION_START` (`300`, first=0, last=0). Each accepted `COMMAND_ACK` means command acceptance, not completed takeoff or landing; fresh aircraft state must corroborate it. [ArduPilot AUTO behavior](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/auto-mode.html) · [MAVLink command protocol](https://mavlink.io/en/services/command.html)

## Current guarded behavior and its limits

1. Bind one reviewed storyboard revision, one flight-controller identity and one serial-link generation to a fresh full mission readback. Compare all executable items, not only the upload ACK. Refuse another authored mission or an uncertain partial upload. The mission's seq 0 is ArduPilot home, not TAKEOFF. The current read-only storage verifier also rejects a zero HOME and requires its horizontal position to be within 10 m of fresh, source-bound aircraft position. This proves proximity only; re-read after power cycling or reconnecting and separately establish actual home/EKF readiness. Props-on field connect, plan review, and readback are available without enabling upload; upload still requires physical props-off confirmation. [MAVLink mission protocol and ArduPilot exceptions](https://mavlink.io/en/services/mission.html)
2. The field check requires one fresh, unsigned MAVLink 2 ArduCopter 4.7.1 quad, disarmed/on ground and near home; GPS/position with HDOP at most 1.4; fresh absolute horizontal and vertical EKF position plus vertical velocity; 4S battery at least 15.2 V; fresh receiver channels, minimum throttle and inactive RC6 stop. It reads critical parameters before issuing a short-lived proof: no skipped arming checks, Quad frame, `AUTO_OPTIONS=0`, RC-loss action **LAND** (`FS_THR_ENABLE=3`), a valid RC throttle-failsafe threshold below `RC3_MIN`, active EKF LAND action, 14.0 V/650 mAh low-battery LAND with a positive delay of at most 10 seconds, and the reviewed mode/RC6 settings. It does **not** change these values. The physically reachable Zorro switch positions, GPS/compass behavior, logs and pilot qualification remain human checks. `MAV_CMD_RUN_PREARM_CHECKS` ACK merely confirms delivery: Copter 4.7.1 returns ACCEPTED even when the checks fail. Lucidity rejects fresh `PreArm:`/`Arm:` warnings when received, but a normal non-forced ARM is still the controller's authoritative check. A GPS fix alone does not prove AUTO navigation readiness. [ArduPilot pre-arm checks](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-prearm-safety-checks.html) · [MAVLink EKF flags](https://mavlink.io/en/messages/ardupilotmega.html#EKF_STATUS_FLAGS)
3. With the aircraft disarmed and on the ground, the service resets to first executable item (`seq=1`) with `MAV_CMD_DO_SET_MISSION_CURRENT` (`224`, reset=0), then requires a correlated ACK and fresh `MISSION_CURRENT=1` before ARM. This avoids silently relying on `MIS_RESTART` after a prior mission or pilot takeover. Fresh-wipe SITL confirmed reset and a second flight after new readback/proof. [MAVLink mission-current semantics](https://mavlink.io/en/services/mission.html)
4. The service owns one physical run at a time. It correlates reset, non-forced ARM and START with source/target-matched ACKs and fresh current-item, armed/AUTO-heartbeat and post-arm EKF evidence. It does not blindly retry a missing ACK; unknown outcomes lock out another launch. The decoder retains source-bound mission, landed-state and ACK events. The run journal is written before commands, retained on unknown or command-bearing rejection, and recovered only after an explicit **props-off**, fresh disarmed/on-ground/near-home observation. A landed run or rejection before any command clears the journal. While a physical run is active, other aircraft writes and voluntary disconnect are blocked. These paths have injected service/middleware checks; the full service-to-real-radio flight has not been tested. During landing Copter may emit `MISSION_CURRENT` 3→1 before the disarmed heartbeat; the observer defers only this near-ground final-LAND reset and demands a newer disarmed heartbeat. Airborne reset remains **unknown**. `MISSION_CURRENT.total=3` in SITL excludes HOME despite four stored items.
5. The UI **Land** action sends `MAV_CMD_DO_SET_MODE` (`176`, custom-mode flag=1, Copter LAND=9) only for a fresh active AUTO run, then requires ACK and LAND-mode heartbeat; it never disarms airborne motors. A lost Mac/DroneBridge link is **unknown**, not a successful stop. Lucidity sends **no GCS heartbeat**, so `FS_GCS_ENABLE` readback does not establish an automatic link-loss LAND trigger. The pilot's verified RC mode switch and onboard failsafes remain essential. RC6 emergency motor stop cuts thrust and is a last resort, not a normal in-air abort. [ArduPilot AUTO takeover](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/auto-mode.html) · [Radio failsafe](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/radio-failsafe.html) · [GCS failsafe](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/gcs-failsafe.html)
6. The observer marks **landed**, not “timeline complete,” only after the LAND item, prior flight progress/airborne evidence, fresh on-ground `EXTENDED_SYS_STATE`, a disarmed heartbeat and near-ground position. The pilot must confirm the first physical landing independently. Copter's final LAND handler need not emit `MISSION_ITEM_REACHED(3)` or `MISSION_STATE_COMPLETE`. [ArduPilot landing behavior](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/land-mode.html)

## Physical blockers

The real quad has only a pilot-reported Stabilize hover and landing. AltHold and Loiter have not been reported as flown; the physically reachable CH5 mode positions and pilot takeover have not been verified. GPS/compass, flight logs, battery behavior under load and onboard failsafe setup need review. The field gate requires `FS_THR_ENABLE=3` (RC-loss LAND), so a vehicle still at Copter's default `1` (RTL) will be blocked until the pilot intentionally configures and verifies it. The service sends no GCS heartbeat, so GCS-link failsafe behavior must not be assumed; adding one would itself change failure behavior. Lucidity has not uploaded/read back this mission on the disarmed physical aircraft with props removed. Its unsigned MAVLink/DroneBridge path has not been flight-qualified. A heartbeat alone does not authenticate the flight controller. There is no downward height sensor to prove exact clearance above ground. [ArduPilot AUTO guidance](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/auto-mode.html)

The guarded Run control is now implemented but must remain unused on the actual quad until the pilot checks, props-off aircraft mission readback and field evidence pass. Simulator transaction success, fixture-level service/UI checks and a passing software preflight cannot by themselves establish physical flight readiness. The later out-and-back storyboard needs a separate compiler and positioning evaluation.
